Hi! I am a Senior Applied Scientist at AWS Security Analytics and AI Research, where I develop AI systems for cloud security. My research focuses on foundation models, autonomous AI agents, and large-scale machine learning systems for threat detection, threat intelligence, and automated security investigations. I am particularly interested in building AI systems that can reason, learn, and collaborate to solve complex cybersecurity problems.

Before joining AWS, I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from MIT, where I was advised by Prof. [Elfar Adalsteinsson](https://www.rle.mit.edu/people/directory/elfar-adalsteinsson/) and worked closely with Prof. [Polina Golland](https://people.csail.mit.edu/polina/) and Prof. [P. Ellen Grant](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=W4dqZ7EAAAAJ). My doctoral research focused on machine learning for medical image analysis, developing robust and efficient algorithms for motion-robust 3D reconstruction, image quality assessment, and fetal MRI analysis.

During my Ph.D., I also interned at Google and Meta, where I worked on machine learning for online advertising and large-scale recommendation systems. Prior to MIT, I received my Bachelor's degree in Engineering Physics from Tsinghua University and spent a summer as a research assistant at Stanford University, working with Prof. [John Pauly](https://web.stanford.edu/~pauly/) and Prof. [Greg Zaharchuk](https://profiles.stanford.edu/greg-zaharchuk).
